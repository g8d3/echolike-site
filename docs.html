EchoLike Lite · Install guide

← Back to download

1. Install the extension

  1. Download the zip and unzip it into a folder you keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions, Brave: brave://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked, pick the folder.

Firefox: about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox → Load Temporary Add-on → pick manifest.json inside the folder (Firefox removes it when it closes).

2. That is it — like a post on X

Your first like creates your own private inbox space on ours and the capture lands there. There is no address to type, no token to copy, no account to create. Free quota: 3000 captures a month.

Click the EchoLike icon to see the last 30 captures, or Open my inbox for the full list in your browser.

3. Optional: your own inbox

Nothing to configure, but if you want your likes only on your own machine, download server.py and run it:

python3 server.py

Then press Find my inbox in the popup: it prefers a server on this computer over the hosted one. You own likes.jsonl, nothing leaves your network.

What we can see: nothing

Every capture is encrypted in your browser before it is sent (AES-256-GCM), and the key never leaves the device. The inbox only stores sealed blobs: our server cannot read your likes, and neither could anyone who broke into it. A stolen inbox key buys ciphertext.

That also means the key is yours to keep: the popup shows your recovery code under Advanced. Save it — it is the only way to read the inbox on another device, and no one can regenerate it for you.

To read your inbox anywhere: Open my inbox in the popup, or open the inbox address with your token and key. Your likes are also mirrored in your own browser, so you can export them without asking us.

Ads

Promoted posts and promoted trends are hidden on X. The switch is in the extension popup: untick Hide ads on X to see them again. Grok answers are never hidden by it. Liking an ad still works and is still captured — it just arrives marked as an ad, never silently dropped.

Phones and automatic updates

The zip never updates itself: a folder has nowhere to look for a new version. If you want the Update button on your browser's extensions page to work, install the packaged build instead: echolike-lite.crx. It carries this page's address as its update source, so Update downloads and installs new versions for you.

Quetta (Android): open the .crx with the browser and confirm. If it answers CRX_REQUIRED_PROOF_MISSING, that browser no longer accepts packages from outside its store: use the zip and repeat step 1 with each new zip.

Without a connection

Captures are always saved in the browser first: click the EchoLike icon and the popup lists the last 30 with a link to each post. Anything that could not be sent waits there and goes out on the next like, on the next start, or with Retry. Nothing is lost.

What it does with your likes

The extension reads the posts you like or bookmark (author, text, link) and sends them to your inbox: the one on your own computer if you run it, the hosted one otherwise. Everything is kept in this browser as well, so the popup works with no connection. There is no EchoLike account. Remove the extension and it stops.

Why Chrome shows a developer-mode warning

EchoLike Lite is not in the Chrome Web Store: the store asks for identity verification. The notice appears for every extension installed this way, it is not a virus alert, and you can close it. The whole source is public, with no build step and no minified code.
