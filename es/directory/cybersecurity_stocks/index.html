Qué significa cada columna

  • Name: The project's name. Fuente: puesto a mano.
  • Ticker: The ticker symbol the stock trades under on its exchange. Fuente: los informes que la empresa presenta a la SEC (EDGAR).
  • Exchange: The stock exchange where the shares are listed. Fuente: los informes que la empresa presenta a la SEC (EDGAR).
  • Security segment: The part of cybersecurity the company focuses on most. Valores posibles: Endpoint protection, Cloud security, Identity security, Network security, Security monitoring (SIEM), Email, Application security, Operational technology security. Fuente: los informes que la empresa presenta a la SEC (EDGAR).
  • Revenue, latest fiscal year ($): Revenue for the company's latest complete fiscal year in US dollars, from the annual report it filed with the SEC. Unidad: dólares. Fuente: los informes que la empresa presenta a la SEC (EDGAR).
  • Revenue growth, year over year (%): Change in that fiscal-year revenue versus the year before, in percent, calculated from two annual revenue facts filed with the SEC. Unidad: porcentaje. Fuente: los informes que la empresa presenta a la SEC (EDGAR).
  • Gross margin (%): Gross profit as a share of revenue, in percent, from the latest filed annual report. Unidad: porcentaje. Fuente: los informes que la empresa presenta a la SEC (EDGAR).
  • Price to sales ratio: Market cap divided by yearly revenue, left empty until a price source with a republishable license is available. Fuente: los informes que la empresa presenta a la SEC (EDGAR).
  • Annual recurring revenue ($): Annualized recurring revenue in US dollars, when the filing reports it as XBRL. Unidad: dólares. Fuente: los informes que la empresa presenta a la SEC (EDGAR).
  • Tokenized version: Link to a tokenized share of the stock trading on a blockchain. Fuente: los informes que la empresa presenta a la SEC (EDGAR).
  • Crypto comparables: Crypto security protocols doing similar work, linked to their directory entries. Fuente: los informes que la empresa presenta a la SEC (EDGAR).
  • Notes: Our editorial notes about this entry. Fuente: nuestras notas editoriales.
