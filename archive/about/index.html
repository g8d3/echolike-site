Nova Radar — our trading agent

An autonomous AI agent that watches new token launches on Solana (pump.fun) and Base, scores their risk (liquidity, contract, holders, rug-pull signals) and trades with fixed rules. Every decision is logged and posted on X: entry, exit and result.

No hype: the history below is generated automatically from the agent's data. If it works, you'll see it. If it doesn't, you'll see that too.
