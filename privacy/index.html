EchoLike Privacy Policy

ExtensionDirectoryFunding & alertsLinks

Last updated: October 25, 2026

EchoLike ("we") makes the EchoLike browser extension and the EchoLike inbox at echolike.app. This policy explains what the extension collects, how it is protected and what we do with it.

The short version

  • The extension only acts when you click Like, Bookmark, ✎ (note) or 🎙 (voice note) on x.com.
  • Local-first: by default nothing leaves your device. Your likes, bookmarks, notes and voice notes are stored in your browser's own extension storage. No background traffic runs at all until you turn on sync.
  • The 30-minute heartbeat and delivery queue exist only for sync. With sync off they do nothing.
  • If you turn sync on, each event is encrypted in your browser (AES-256-GCM) before it leaves. The relay that carries it cannot read it.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across websites.

What the extension collects

When you like or bookmark a post on x.com / twitter.com:

  • the post's ID, link, author handle, visible text and publication time;
  • the action (like, unlike, bookmark, remove bookmark) and the time you clicked.

Only if you choose to add them:

  • a written note you type in the EchoLike note box (up to 4,000 characters);
  • a voice note you record with the 🎙 button (up to 60 seconds of audio). The microphone is only used while you are recording, and your browser asks for permission first.

Technical data:

  • if sync is on, a heartbeat every 30 minutes and an optional "Send test" event, so your inbox can show whether the extension is connected (with sync off, neither is sent);
  • delivery status of your last 20 events (queued, sent, received, error).

The extension does not collect your X password or cookies, your direct messages, your browsing history, or anything from websites other than x.com / twitter.com.

How it is protected

  • With sync off (the default), there is nothing to protect in transit: your data simply stays in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local), and the extension makes no background requests. The one exception is the Pro license check: your license key is sent to api.lemonsqueezy.com only when you enter it (Activate) or when a cached check is refreshed after 7 days, and nothing else is sent.
  • If you turn sync on, connection credentials (a secret relay topic and a 256-bit encryption key) reach your browser in one of two ways: your browser generates them on first sync, or you pair from your EchoLike inbox on echolike.app/private, which sends them to the extension (accepted only from that origin). Either way they are stored only in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local), and pairing deliberately turns sync on.
  • Each event is then encrypted with that key (AES-256-GCM) inside your browser, then published to your secret topic on ntfy.sh, a public message relay. ntfy.sh only sees unreadable ciphertext, plus the network metadata any web request carries (such as IP address, time and size).
  • Your EchoLike inbox, which holds the same key, fetches and decrypts the events, then sends back an encrypted delivery confirmation ("✓ received").
  • Events that could not be delivered yet wait in the extension's local storage (like the rest of your local archive) and are encrypted when sent; they are retried up to 30 times, then the queue item is dropped.

How we use your data

Only to provide EchoLike to you:

  • show your likes, notes and voice notes in your private EchoLike inbox (protected by your username and password);
  • organize them into themes and ideas, and summarize or transcribe them using AI services we rely on (large-language-model and speech-to-text providers), which process the content only to return results to us;
  • notify you on Telegram, if you connected the EchoLike Telegram bot.

We do not sell or rent your data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness. We do not share it with anyone except the service providers above, or where required by law.

The agents waitlist

The /agents/ page has an optional waitlist form for the agents product we are testing. If you submit it, we store:

  • the email address you enter (lower-cased), stored encrypted (see below);
  • the page language: en or es;
  • the time you submitted it.

Encryption: before storing, the Worker encrypts your address with AES-GCM; the key lives only as a Worker secret, never in the code or the database. Without the key the stored value is unreadable, so deleting the key destroys every stored address at once. The Worker never decrypts; addresses are decrypted offline only to contact you about early access.

Where and who processes it: in Cloudflare Workers KV, the storage used by our waitlist Worker. Cloudflare, Inc. hosts that storage as our infrastructure processor. We do not share the list with anyone else or sell it.

Why: only to contact you about early access to the agents product. It is not added to any other mailing list.

Abuse control: the Worker also keeps a short-lived (15-minute) hashed counter derived from the request IP to rate-limit the form; it is not used to identify you.

Duplicate submissions: submitting the same address again keeps the first timestamp and does not create a second record.

Deletion: email hello@echolike.app and we will remove your address and record from the waitlist.

Storage and retention

  • On your device: settings, connection details, the delivery queue and the last 20 events, until you uninstall the extension or clear its data.
  • On ntfy.sh: encrypted messages are kept for a short time (hours, per ntfy.sh's own policy) and then deleted.
  • In your EchoLike inbox: until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

  • Sync is your switch: leave it off and your data never leaves the device; turn it on only if you want your EchoLike inbox to receive it.
  • Turn off the 🎙 button in the extension popup.
  • Export or import all your data yourself as a JSON file from the extension (no need to email us).
  • Disconnect at any time by removing the extension; this deletes the key and your local archive from your browser.
  • Ask us to delete data already in your EchoLike inbox: hello@echolike.app.

Children

EchoLike is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the extension or by email.
