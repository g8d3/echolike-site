EchoLike Liquidity

Market-making setup for new token teams

We set up and run an automated market maker for your token's trading pair, so there are buy and sell orders on the book from day one. It runs on your own exchange account and API keys. Funds never leave your account and we never hold them.

What you get

  • Setup: open-source Hummingbot configured for your pair, spreads, order sizes and inventory limits.
  • Monitoring: we watch uptime, open orders, spreads and inventory.
  • Alerts: you get a message when the bot stops, inventory runs low or the spread drifts.

No profit guarantee. Market making can lose money, including through price moves and inventory risk. This is a setup and monitoring service, not investment advice, and results are not promised. Check that market making is allowed for your token where you operate.
