What each column means

  • Model: The model's name, as the OpenCode Go plan lists it. Source: entered by hand.
  • Plan page: The page where the plan states this model's limits (opencode.ai/go). Source: entered by hand.
  • Requests per 5 hours on the OpenCode Go plan: How many requests this model allows in a 5-hour window on the OpenCode Go plan ($10 a month), as the plan page states it. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Monthly usage value on the OpenCode Go plan ($): How much model usage, in US dollars, the OpenCode Go plan ($10 a month) gives for this model each month. Unit: US dollars. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Requests per 5 hours on the OpenCode Go Plus plan: How many requests this model allows in a 5-hour window on the OpenCode Go Plus plan ($40 a month), as the plan page states it. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Monthly usage value on the OpenCode Go Plus plan ($): How much model usage, in US dollars, the OpenCode Go Plus plan ($40 a month) gives for this model each month. Unit: US dollars. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Answer speed (tokens per second): Our own measurement: output tokens per second while the model was writing, over 3 timed runs of the same 500-token prompt with no tools, on the EchoLike mini PC. Measured by us, not a vendor figure. Source: measured by us on the EchoLike mini PC: timed pi runs, no vendor figure.
  • Time to first token (milliseconds): Our own measurement: milliseconds until the model wrote its first token, median of 3 timed runs of the same prompt, no tools, on the EchoLike mini PC. Source: measured by us on the EchoLike mini PC: timed pi runs, no vendor figure.
  • Failed or empty answers (%): Our own measurement: share of the 3 timed runs in which the model failed, timed out or gave no answer, in percent. Unit: percent. Source: measured by us on the EchoLike mini PC: timed pi runs, no vendor figure.
  • Notes: Our editorial notes about this entry. Source: our editorial notes.
