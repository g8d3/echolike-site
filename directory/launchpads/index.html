What each column means

  • Name: The project's name. Source: entered by hand.
  • Website: The project's official website. Source: entered by hand.
  • Blockchains: The blockchains it supports. Possible values: Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, BNB Chain, Polygon, Hyperliquid, Sui, Aptos, TON, Tron, Bitcoin, Avalanche, Starknet, zkSync, Linea, Blast, Monad, Berachain. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Launch fee: What it costs to launch a token, in US dollars. Unit: US dollars. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Trade fee (bps): Fee charged on each trade, in basis points; 1 bps is 0.01%. Unit: basis points. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Creator fee (bps): Share of each trade paid to the token creator, in basis points. Unit: basis points. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Graduation market cap ($): Market cap at which a token moves from the launchpad to an open market, in US dollars. Unit: US dollars. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Identity check required (KYC): Yes when you must verify your identity before you can use it. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Public API: Yes when it offers a documented public API. Source: the project's own pages, read by AI and checked against our rules.
  • Notes: Our editorial notes about this entry. Source: our editorial notes.
