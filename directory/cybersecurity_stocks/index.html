What each column means

  • Name: The project's name. Source: entered by hand.
  • Ticker: The ticker symbol the stock trades under on its exchange. Source: the filings the company submits to the SEC (EDGAR).
  • Exchange: The stock exchange where the shares are listed. Source: the filings the company submits to the SEC (EDGAR).
  • Security segment: The part of cybersecurity the company focuses on most. Possible values: Endpoint protection, Cloud security, Identity security, Network security, Security monitoring (SIEM), Email, Application security, Operational technology security. Source: the filings the company submits to the SEC (EDGAR).
  • Revenue, latest fiscal year ($): Revenue for the company's latest complete fiscal year in US dollars, from the annual report it filed with the SEC. Unit: US dollars. Source: the filings the company submits to the SEC (EDGAR).
  • Revenue growth, year over year (%): Change in that fiscal-year revenue versus the year before, in percent, calculated from two annual revenue facts filed with the SEC. Unit: percent. Source: the filings the company submits to the SEC (EDGAR).
  • Gross margin (%): Gross profit as a share of revenue, in percent, from the latest filed annual report. Unit: percent. Source: the filings the company submits to the SEC (EDGAR).
  • Price to sales ratio: Market cap divided by yearly revenue, left empty until a price source with a republishable license is available. Source: the filings the company submits to the SEC (EDGAR).
  • Annual recurring revenue ($): Annualized recurring revenue in US dollars, when the filing reports it as XBRL. Unit: US dollars. Source: the filings the company submits to the SEC (EDGAR).
  • Tokenized version: Link to a tokenized share of the stock trading on a blockchain. Source: the filings the company submits to the SEC (EDGAR).
  • Crypto comparables: Crypto security protocols doing similar work, linked to their directory entries. Source: the filings the company submits to the SEC (EDGAR).
  • Notes: Our editorial notes about this entry. Source: our editorial notes.
