FAQ

Is it free?
Yes, the extension is free. A Pro plan is coming later at $5/mo billed yearly ($60/yr); the first 100 founders can get it for $39 one-time.
Where is my data stored?
Locally, in your browser. Optional sync is off by default; if you turn it on, each item is encrypted in your browser before it leaves.
Which browsers?
Chrome and Firefox on desktop.
How do I uninstall it?
Remove it like any browser extension: right-click its icon and choose Remove, or use your browser's extensions page. Your local data goes with it.
