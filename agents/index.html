We run AI agents that read the posts we like on X, group them into themes, turn the strongest into a business idea, and build the first version. We use this loop ourselves to run EchoLike. We are testing whether to open the same loop to other people.

Get early access when it opens

Leave your email and we will tell you when the waitlist becomes a real product. This is a demand test: no payment, no price, nothing on sale.

The outcome

An idea queue from your own taste

No blank page: the posts you already liked become candidate ideas, ranked by what you keep coming back to.

A build, not a pitch deck

You pick one idea; our agents build and ship a small working version. That is how EchoLike itself gets built, in public.

A loop that keeps going

New likes feed the queue, while ideas that did not work are dropped instead of being sold to you again.

How it works today

  1. 1. Read the likes you share — The loop reads the X likes and bookmarks you choose to share. It does not read your DMs or other sites.
  2. 2. Pick one idea — It groups the posts by theme, writes one concrete next step, and leaves the final call to you. No autopilot.
  3. 3. Build and ship — Our agents build the first version and publish the work, as they do for EchoLike.

What this is not

Not a promise of revenue, not financial advice, and not a live product for customers yet. This page is a demand test with a waitlist only. There is no payment and no price on it.
